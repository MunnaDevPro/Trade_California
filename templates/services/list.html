Professional Services for International Trade

International business requires more than simply finding a product or supplier. Trade California provides support to help businesses better understand, communicate, and operate within the U.S. trade environment.

U.S. Logistics Support

International trade requires careful coordination between suppliers, buyers, shipping companies, ports, customs authorities, and logistics partners. Our logistics support may include:

  • Supplier & shipment coordination
  • Export logistics communication
  • Port & shipping coordination
  • Freight & transportation support
  • Documentation coordination
  • International shipment communication

Our goal is to help make the movement of products from the United States to international markets more efficient and organized.

International Business Consultancy

Entering a new international market can be challenging. Trade California provides business consultancy support for companies seeking to:

  • Identify international business opportunities
  • Explore U.S. products & suppliers
  • Develop international partnerships
  • Understand market opportunities
  • Connect with buyers & distributors
  • Develop cross-border business strategies

We help businesses transform international opportunities into practical business relationships.
